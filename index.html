About the Game

Needle Storm is a cheerful offline timing game where you launch needles into a spinning pet wheel, avoid collisions, and chase the cleanest clear. Each level is quick, focused, and easy to restart, with playful cat-and-dog visuals designed for relaxing but tense arcade sessions.

10 handcrafted-feeling progressive levels
Three-miss scoring system for fair retries
Cat-and-dog themed buttons, stars, and effects
Fully offline play with saved level progress
