Privacy Policy

Needle Storm respects your privacy.

Game Introduction Developer

Overview

Needle Storm is a single-player offline mobile game. The game is designed to be played without account registration and without requiring players to submit personal information to the developer.

Information Collection

The game stores gameplay progress, settings, level unlocks, and star records locally on your device. This data is used only to provide the game experience and can be reset in the game settings.

The game template may include third-party advertising, analytics, security, or platform SDK components. These services may process limited device, diagnostic, advertising, or usage information according to their own privacy practices when enabled in a release build.

Use of Information

Locally stored game data is used to save progress, remember sound and music preferences, and improve gameplay continuity. The developer does not sell personal information.

Children's Privacy

Needle Storm is a casual game suitable for general audiences. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information, please contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.
